Risk limits

Limits come from the pool, not from a committee

Each rule below runs on every order. None of them can be turned off for a single trader.

Leverage tracks depth

A market's cap is set by how much capital sits within 2% of the current price. Deep books get 20×. A coin with a $400k pool gets 3×.

depth ≥ $50M → 20× · ≥ $5M → 10× · else 3–5×

Smoothed mark price

Liquidations check a time-weighted average, so one wick on a thin pool can't close a healthy position.

mark = 30s TWAP of pool price

Drift breaker

If spot runs more than 8% away from the mark, new positions pause. Closing and adding margin stay open the whole time.

|spot − mark| / mark > 8% → pause opens

Impact you can see

Larger orders pay a premium that grows with size relative to depth. The ticket shows it before you submit.

impact = size² / (depth × 400)

Open-interest caps

Each side of a market has its own ceiling, set as a share of pool depth. When a side is full, only the other side can open.

OI cap per side = 15% of depth

Payout ceiling

On small-cap markets a single position can win at most 4× its margin. This keeps a slow pump from draining the vault.

tier 3 max payout = 4× margin

Vault

Take the other side of every trade

The vault is the counterparty. Depositors earn trading fees and funding, and absorb trader profits. In this demo the numbers are illustrative.

  • Fees go to depositors. 70% of open and close fees accrue to the vault share price.
  • Withdrawals are rate-limited. Up to 10% of TVL can leave per day so open positions stay backed.
  • Exposure is capped. Net open interest can't exceed 40% of vault assets.

FAQ

Questions

Is any of this real money?

No. Kestrel is a paper-trading demo. Prices are simulated in your browser and the balance resets whenever you press Reset. Nothing connects to a wallet or a chain.

How is the liquidation price calculated?

A position is liquidated when its remaining margin falls below 1% of its size. For a long that price is entry × (1 − 1/leverage + 0.01). For a short it is entry × (1 + 1/leverage − 0.01).

Why can't I use 20× on every coin?

High leverage on a thin pool lets a small amount of capital push the price into everyone's liquidation levels. Caps scale with depth so that attack costs more than it pays.

What is funding?

An hourly payment between longs and shorts that pulls the market back toward balance. When longs outweigh shorts, longs pay. The demo shows the rate but does not charge it.

Where is my data stored?

Your demo balance and positions are kept in this browser only. Clearing site data removes them.
